# Contributions statement (SZL side, draft for the alignment conversation)

Authorship and contributions to be agreed BEFORE drafting, per the
correspondence ("much easier to do now, while neither of us knows what the
numbers will be"). This is the SZL side's draft, offered for amendment.

## Design contributions (Lutar, SZL Holdings)

1. **The four governance concepts** — states with precedence (with the
   empty denominator resolving to unavailable), the coverage identity as
   a gate, the handoff fields naming what a result does not prove, and
   staleness propagating to descendants — published on the Claude
   Science forum in September 2026; adopted by the Vidal-Gadea lab in a
   single ruling on 30 September 2026 (per the lab's own public
   correction).
2. **The scoring-harness scaffolding** implementing those concepts
   (versioned, hash-pinned, reviewable; formal-ai-underwriting
   `harness/scoring/`), offered for the lab's review before any arm runs.
3. **The shared fixture package** — the six failure classes named in the
   correspondence as synthetic, rights-cleared, provenance-carrying
   fixtures with declared expected verdicts
   (`harness/scoring/fixtures/`).
4. **The symmetric scoring design** for unsupported inference against
   missed constraints (to be finalized in the protocol draft).
5. **The pre-registration deposit infrastructure** (versioned package,
   canonical form, SHA-256, provenance, public DOI).

## Agent-work disclosure (both sides)

Work on the SZL side was performed with AI assistance under human
direction. The harness, the fixture package, the tests, and the receipts
are machine-checkable artifacts of that assistance. The paper should
describe this lane as it describes the lab's agent lanes — the methods
section cannot be coy about agent work without refuting its own argument.

## Scope boundaries (agreed, restated)

- Fixture schemas and protocol text cross in both directions, rights
  cleared, source revision on each side.
- Nothing of the lab's recordings, unpublished values, or student work
  crosses in either direction; the biological gates stay with the lab.
- Passing synthetic fixtures demonstrates gate behavior only — nothing
  about any biological measurement (the reciprocal honesty clause).
- Machine-second-rater agreement is reported as agreement, never as
  independent proof that scoring is correct.
